# Changelog

## Installer 1.2 (pack 3)

- Everything happens in the installer's window - no more console. It shows each download and install step with a progress bar (Show details lists every file and where it goes), and problems with what to do and Try again.
- The window stays open after Install, Repair, Uninstall, Uninstall all and Turn off / on: it comes back with your mods as they are now, and what was done at the top. Only Close, the X and Play close it.
- "Where your mods are" at the top: which mods are installed, where, their exact folder, and how to play them.
- The green button says what it will do - Install, Update, Remove, Move, Apply changes, or Up to date - and the line above it spells it out. Each install location shows its folder; picking another one says the mods will move there.
- Uninstall (one mod or all) asks first and says exactly what goes, and from where.
- Hover over anything for an explanation. Help (top right) and each ? open a guide inside the installer, with pictures.
- Uninstall.bat runs in the same window, and asks first.
- When Plutonium doesn't say where Black Ops 2 is, the window asks (with Steam's folder to pick, if it finds one).
- Installers from earlier packs update themselves to 1.2 the next time they run.

## Installer 1.1 (pack 2)

- Uninstalling keeps the installer and its "MaliceDevelops BO2 Mods" shortcut (one mod or all of them), so you can install again from the shortcut.
- Installers from pack 1 update themselves to 1.1 the next time they run.

## Installer 1.0 (pack 1)

First release. One installer for all of my mods: tick the ones you want, and only those are downloaded and installed.

- Into Zombies Declassified, alongside it as a second Mods menu entry (your own stays untouched), or as a mod of their own (without Zombies Declassified).
- Stays on your PC: a "MaliceDevelops BO2 Mods" shortcut on the desktop and in the Start menu opens it again.
- Settings window for each mod, with Reset to defaults.
- Repair: checks every installed file and downloads again only what's missing or damaged.
- Turn off / Turn on: Zombies Declassified plays as it came, then the mods come back without downloading again.
- Play: opens Plutonium's launcher.
- Updates itself when a newer installer is out.
- The console window closes with the installer; it stays open only after an error, so you can read it.

- [Upgraded Cymbal Monkey 1.0](https://github.com/MaliceDevelops/upgraded-cymbal-monkey-bo2)
